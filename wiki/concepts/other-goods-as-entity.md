# OTHER GOODS as an Entity

## Idea

The user proposed creating an artist persona that operates mysteriously through GitHub, Instagram, and YouTube accounts. The user also proposed the long-term possibility of adding service interfaces such as Delivery, Markets, and Maps outside Commerce within the same world.

## Developed Direction

An LLM proposed presenting OTHER GOODS not as “an artist hiding their identity” but as **a commercial organization or system that exists without an artist**. GitHub would speak as a software organization, Instagram as a store account, and YouTube as a product channel. These channels would offer paths for further discovery rather than explanations of the work. This specific interpretation has `attribution: llm-proposed` and is not a confirmed user decision.

## Why Deferred

Under [[DEC-004-commerce-first]], the current priority is to create the concept and experience of Commerce itself. The artist persona, social channels, GitHub world, and later services will be reconsidered after Commerce stands as an independent work.
