# Time as Currency

## Idea

The user proposed implementing an internal OTHER GOODS currency on a blockchain and naming its unit `TIME`. The user then proposed a system in which products are paid for in dollars while the value between `TIME` and dollars changes in real time.

## Why Deferred

As the discussion expanded into a token, exchange, and ownership system, the user judged that it had strayed too far and chose to return to [[conceptual-contrast]]. Blockchain and a `TIME` economy are therefore not part of the current central system. Time may remain product material, but OTHER GOODS is not fixed as a project exclusively about time.

## Revisit Condition

This criterion has `attribution: llm-synthesis`.

Reconsider tokenization when it becomes essential to the work's current questions—such as the impossibility of ownership or the arbitrariness of value—and when a concrete Commerce product cannot function without the technology.
