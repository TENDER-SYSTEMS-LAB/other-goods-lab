# DEC-003 — OTHER GOODS Identity

## Status

accepted (2026-09-03)

## Decision

- Working project/store name: **OTHER GOODS**
- Customer-facing tagline: **A store for other kinds of goods.**
- Project statement seed: **Treating the unownable as merchandise.**

The user selected `OTHER GOODS` as a strong name and judged it suitable for possible expansion into physical goods. The user then directly assigned the roles of the two phrases.

## Rationale

`OTHER GOODS` first reads as a normal store or brand, then rereads after the experience as “a place that treats things that are not goods as goods.” The tagline does not close the work's meaning in advance. The project statement seed provides a starting point for artist statements or exhibition introductions that develop questions of ownership, price, and exchange.

## Consequences

- Distinguish customer-facing language from project and research language.
- Before release, separately check domain names, social handles, GitHub organizations, and trademark availability. This research has not yet been done.
