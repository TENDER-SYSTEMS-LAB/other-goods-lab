# DEC-004 — Commerce First

## Status

accepted (2026-09-03)

## Context

The conversation considered later expansion from Commerce into Delivery, Markets, and Maps; an anonymous artist persona; a GitHub, Instagram, and YouTube world; blockchain and tokenization; and a `TIME` economy. As the scope grew, the user decided to build Commerce properly first.

## Decision

Focus the first work on **OTHER GOODS / Commerce**. Defer other services, world-building channels, the artist persona, and blockchain or tokenization until Commerce stands as an independent work or a concrete need emerges.

## Immediate Focus

The following sequence is an `attribution: llm-synthesis` application of the Commerce-first decision to the current conversation:

- Develop a product system around collisions between things that cannot be products and commerce functions.
- Test the four product candidates and additional candidates before confirming the first collection.
- Decide on real checkout, payment, and post-order experience only after defining the properties of the products.
